Wolf range repair essentials

We service Wolf dual-fuel and gas ranges, rangetops, and sealed burners. Our technicians restore ignition, heat balance, and control performance to factory standards.

Typical problems

  • Burners won’t ignite or keep clicking
  • Uneven heating or oven not reaching temp
  • Griddle, charbroiler, or infrared not responding
  • Control panel, probe, or display errors
  • Gas smell, ignition delay, or safety shutoff issues
  • Fan noise, cooling, or convection issues
